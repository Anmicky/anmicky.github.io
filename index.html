Research interests

Where I work

AI × Cognitive Science

How memory distorts, and what that teaches machines

Generative AI and representation learning meet perception, memory and decision-making. My first-author work asks which dimensions of real objects are fragile in memory — and why.

Robust, human-aligned AI

Feeding cognitive vulnerability research back into AI

Robustness, representation bias, and human-in-the-loop design: systems that understand human perceptual limits, not just benchmark scores.

Biomedical & health

From cognitive mechanisms to clinical systems

Applying AI to medical imaging and high-dimensional bio data, inspired by how healthy and fragile cognition actually works.
